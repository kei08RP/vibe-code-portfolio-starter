About

My journal

I’m a multidisciplinary designer and developer with a soft spot for tactile visuals, bold colours, and interfaces that invite people to explore. My work blends storytelling, systems thinking, and clean front-end craft to turn ideas into memorable experiences.

I learned to love design by making things by hand first: moodboards, sketches, prototypes, and tiny experiments. That same playful curiosity follows me into every website, product, and interaction I build.
